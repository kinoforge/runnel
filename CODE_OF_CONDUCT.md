# Code of conduct

Runnel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to oss@kinoforge.dev. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or ban participants who break these rules, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
